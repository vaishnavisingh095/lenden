Build ONLY the first screen of my startup prototype called "Lenden".

Lenden is a voice-first business memory and payment-collection assistant for traditional Indian B2B business owners. The user should be able to quickly tell Lenden what happened with a customer, without filling complicated forms.

IMPORTANT:
This is ONLY the input screen for now.
Do NOT build:
- database
- authentication
- WhatsApp integration
- reminders
- dashboards
- analytics
- payment processing
- collection prioritization
- customer profiles
- backend
- AI extraction
- Sarvam integration

DESIGN GOAL:
The user is a busy business owner who may not be comfortable with complicated apps. The screen must feel simpler than Khatabook, Excel, or a traditional CRM.

Make it:
- extremely simple
- mobile-first
- clean
- professional
- trustworthy
- easy for an older business owner to understand
- large buttons and readable text
- minimal text
- no unnecessary animations
- no complicated navigation
